The Call for Papers for link:https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-europe/co-located-events/keycloakcon/[KeycloakCon Europe 2027] is now open!

KeycloakCon Europe 2027 is a half-day, single-track conference in Barcelona on March 15, 2027 — co-located with KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe.
After successful events in Amsterdam and Yokohama, KeycloakCon is back with talks, discussions, and community connections.

== Why submit a talk?

* *Full-access KubeCon pass* — Accepted speakers receive a full-access pass to KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe and all co-located events.
* *Reach the Keycloak community* — Present to a focused audience of Keycloak users, contributors, and maintainers who are there specifically for identity and access management.
* *Barcelona in March* — A great city to combine with the broader KubeCon experience running March 15-18.

== What we'd love to hear about

We are looking for a range of topics. Here is what the community is curious about — but don't limit yourself to this list:

* How you deployed or migrated to Keycloak and what you learned along the way
* Keycloak and AI — securing agents, MCP servers, or delegating access for automated workflows
* Authentication standards in practice — Passkeys, OpenID Connect, Verifiable Credentials, or EU Digital Identity Wallet
* Operating Keycloak at scale — upgrades, high availability, multi-cluster setups, and automation
* Integrations with other CNCF projects and cloud-native infrastructure
* Automating identity lifecycle with Workflows, SCIM provisioning, or Shared Signals
* Compliance, audit, and governance with Keycloak

If you are unsure whether your idea fits, submit it! We would rather see a creative proposal than miss out on a great talk.

== Session formats

* *Presentation* (25 min, 1-2 speakers) — Deep dives, case studies, or feature walkthroughs.
* *Panel Discussion* (35 min, 3-5 speakers from at least 3 organizations) — Moderated conversations on cross-cutting topics.
* *Lightning Talk* (10 min, 1 speaker) — Short and focused. A great option if you are speaking at a conference for the first time!

The deadline for submissions is *Sunday, October 18, 2026*.

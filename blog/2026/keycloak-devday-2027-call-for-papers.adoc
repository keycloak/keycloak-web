📣 It's time to share what you've built, learned and fought with! The *Call for Papers for Keycloak DevDay 2027* is open until *October 24, 2026*.

As announced in our link:https://www.keycloak.org/2026/06/keycloak-devday-2027-save-the-date[Save the Date post] back in June, the world's largest Keycloak community conference returns to *Darmstadt, Germany*, on *April 8–9, 2027*.
And once again, we want the conference day to be shaped by the people who know Keycloak best: you, the community.

== What we're looking for 📝

Anything Keycloak-related is welcome! To give you some inspiration, we'd love to hear about:

* Real-world implementations and case studies
* Migration stories and lessons learned – including the things that went wrong
* Keycloak core features and new developments
* Extension development and customization (SPIs, themes, providers, ...)
* Operating Keycloak in production: deployment, monitoring, upgrades
* Performance optimization and scaling
* Security best practices and patterns
* Identity standards and protocols (OpenID Connect, OAuth 2.x, SAML, passkeys, ...)
* Integration with cloud-native technologies

One thing to keep in mind: everyone at DevDay already knows Keycloak.
*Please don't submit an introduction talk.*
Our audience wants to go deep, so bring the details, the code and the war stories.

== How to submit

👉 *Submit your proposal on Sessionize: https://sessionize.com/keycloak-devday-2027/[sessionize.com/keycloak-devday-2027]*

⏰ *Deadline: October 24, 2026*

As a speaker, you get *free access to the event* (main speaker only, not co-speakers).
As a community conference, we keep costs as low as possible, so we can't cover travel or accommodation.
Thanks for your understanding!

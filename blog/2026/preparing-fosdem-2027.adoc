== Meet the community at the Sovereign Identity stand

// Add opening hours and location of the stand once FOSDEM publishes them.

The Keycloak project will co-host the stand "`Sovereign Identity Everywhere: Keycloak, FreeIPA, privacyIDEA, & SSSD`" together with the FreeIPA, privacyIDEA and SSSD projects on both days, Saturday, January 30th and Sunday, January 31st 2027.
True digital sovereignty starts with self-hosted, user-owned identity and access management.
Visit this joint stand to meet the core teams and contributors behind four popular open-source security solutions:

https://www.keycloak.org[Keycloak]:: Extensible, self-hosted Single Sign-On and Identity Provider supporting Passkeys, Digital Wallets, OIDC, OAuth 2.0, SAML, Kerberos, and SCIM.

https://www.freeipa.org[FreeIPA]:: Centralized management for Linux users, hosts, and Kerberos realms, complete with cross-realm trusts and built-in certificate issuance.

https://www.privacyidea.org/[privacyIDEA]:: On-premises central multi-factor authentication (MFA) management, integrating seamlessly with Keycloak, FreeIPA, Linux PAM, Windows logins, and RADIUS using everything from OTP apps to device-bound passkeys.

https://sssd.io/[SSSD]:: The enterprise-grade Linux client enabling machines to securely connect with Active Directory, FreeIPA, or LDAP domains for unified identity, policy enforcement, and authentication.

Whether you're securing a home lab or an enterprise infrastructure, come chat with us about how these tools work individually and how they integrate to form a complete open-source identity stack.

== Submit your talk now

FOSDEM is a big event divided into smaller, single-track conferences with their own call for papers and organizers.
The call for papers is running now, and each devroom has its own deadline between November 27th and December 2nd.
Submit your proposal via https://fosdem.org/submit[FOSDEM's Pretalx system], and select the track of the devroom you are targeting.

=== Identity and Access Management devroom

The https://iam-devroom.github.io/fosdem-2027/[Identity and Access Management devroom] is the natural home for Keycloak talks.
It takes place on Saturday afternoon, January 30th, and the deadline for submissions is *November 30th 2026*.
The devroom is looking for talks on passwordless authentication with passkeys, decentralized identity and verifiable credentials, quantum-resistant cryptography, identity in the software supply chain, improving the user experience of multi-factor authentication, and IAM deployment reports and real-world case studies.
If you have migrated to Keycloak, integrated it with other free software, or built an extension, this is the place to share it.
Talks are 25 minutes, including questions.

=== More devrooms where Keycloak talks fit

Your Keycloak story might fit other devrooms, too.
*Some of these devrooms close their submissions earlier than the IAM devroom, as early as November 27th, so don't wait.*
Check each devroom's call for participation for its topics and its exact deadline.
Deadlines are in parentheses where the devroom announced one.

* How do you run, upgrade, scale and monitor Keycloak in production?
Share your experience in the https://github.com/OperationsDevroom/2027/blob/main/CfP.md[Operations] (Nov 27), https://discuss.linuxcontainers.org/t/fosdem-2027-containers-devroom-call-for-papers/27476[Containers] (Nov 30) or https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bjIIUeA6gTdC0dRwdzGe0S6R1dSA3JwfwrUXV6BVHYg/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.dm1sd8uel8zz[Monitoring and Observability] devrooms.
* Did you build a Keycloak extension, tune its performance, or test your login flows in CI?
Keycloak is built on Quarkus and runs on the JVM, so the https://lists.fosdem.org/hyperkitty/list/fosdem@lists.fosdem.org/message/YKSYJVQKGPPWPX4CFEVHV675DILCQ36H/[Free Java] (Nov 29) and https://lists.fosdem.org/hyperkitty/list/fosdem@lists.fosdem.org/thread/Q7ASYI2PMOXKYZEOOBABNYP7WS2CDYTG/[Testing and Continuous Delivery] (Dec 1) devrooms are a good match.
* Do you harden your identity infrastructure or apply OAuth 2.0 security best practices?
Bring it to the https://lists.fosdem.org/hyperkitty/list/fosdem@lists.fosdem.org/message/RU2KRJHGYVJLT6W6XLUR3S4OW35FQXOK/[Security] devroom (Dec 1).
* Is your organization preparing for the Cyber Resilience Act, or building sovereign infrastructure for the public sector?
Identity is a key part of both, and the https://openssf.org/event/fosdem-2027-beyond-the-cra-building-secure-and-resilient-open-source/[Beyond the CRA] (Nov 27) and https://lists.fosdem.org/hyperkitty/list/fosdem@lists.fosdem.org/message/T7UBYKX5CFGIXBDPVPKKIBHWF72ELF2Z/[Open Source & EU Policy] (Dec 1) devrooms are looking for practical experience.
* Are you working with digital wallets, verifiable credentials or user-controlled identity?
The https://lists.fosdem.org/hyperkitty/list/fosdem@lists.fosdem.org/message/EM6BYJXKWNQ3PAZWDIP5BT3VKF37HL2M/[Decentralized Internet and Privacy] devroom (Dec 2) is a natural fit.

=== FOSDEM Fringe

Around the FOSDEM weekend, independent events take place in Brussels and nearby.
They will be listed on the FOSDEM Fringe page once FOSDEM publishes it.
Some of them have their own calls for papers, and some fit Keycloak well:

* https://2027.fosdempgday.org/[FOSDEM PGDay] on January 28th-29th: Keycloak stores its data in PostgreSQL, so talks about running Keycloak on it could fit.
* https://blogs.oracle.com/mysql/mysql-belgian-days-2027[MySQL Belgian Days] for those running Keycloak on MySQL.
* https://fossapalooza.eu/[FOSSapalooza] on January 29th, the day before FOSDEM.
* https://cfgmgmtcamp.org/ghent2027/[CfgMgmtCamp] in Ghent from February 1st to 3rd, with a focus on configuration management and operations.
* https://opentelemetry.io/blog/2026/otel-unplugged-fosdem-2027/[OTel Unplugged] on February 1st, an OpenTelemetry unconference and roadmapping session, relevant to anyone observing Keycloak with OpenTelemetry.
* The https://workshop.aboutcode.org/[FOSS license and security compliance tools workshop] on February 1st, for software provenance, vulnerability management and regulatory compliance like the CRA.

Not sure where your idea fits?
Post your idea in our https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak/discussions/55426[FOSDEM discussion on GitHub], and we will help you find the right room.
The full list is on the https://fosdem.org/2027/news/accepted-developer-rooms/[accepted developer rooms] page.
